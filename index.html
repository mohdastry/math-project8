The Bioscience Problem

                    

                        In agro-product processing, determining the exact ripening velocity of the **Perlis Harumanis Mango (Mangifera indica)** is crucial. Processing the mango into purees or freeze-dried snacks requires a precise Total Soluble Solids (TSS) content, measured in **°Brix**. 
                    

                    

                        By modeling the sugar accumulation curve, the **first derivative $f'(x)$** tells agro-technologists the exact *rate of sugar accumulation per day*. The peak of this derivative represents the **Inflection Point**—the day of maximum ripening velocity, critical for timing enzymatic deactivation during processing.
                    

                    

                        Data Reference Framework: Based on realistic physiological ripening patterns established by the Malaysian Agricultural Research and Development Institute (MARDI) and the Department of Agriculture (DOA) Malaysia.
                    

                


                

                    
Mathematical Modeling

                    
                    

                        Sugar Content Function, $f(x)$
                        

                            $$f(x) = \frac{L}{1 + e^{-k(x - x_0)}}$$
                        

                        

                            Where $L = 18.0^\circ\text{Brix}$ (Max sweetness), $k = 0.65$ (growth rate constant), and $x_0 = 5.0$ (mid-point day).
                        

                    


                    

                        Accumulation Rate Derivative, $f'(x)$
                        

                            $$f'(x) = \frac{L \cdot k \cdot e^{-k(x - x_0)}}{(1 + e^{-k(x - x_0)})^2}$$
                        

                        

                            Calculates the instantaneous change in sugar content ($\text{Brix/day}$) at exactly day $x$.
